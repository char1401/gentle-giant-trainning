About Us

Founded by Division I and professional football veterans, Gentle Giant Training exists to help the next generation of athletes build confidence, sharpen technique, and prepare for meaningful opportunities beyond high school.

M

Our Mission

To help local athletes earn the same opportunities we were given by offering accessible training to the community that raised us. We teach the ins and outs of college and professional football while building player IQ, technical skill, and a rock-solid foundation of strength, awareness, intelligence, and grit.

V

Our Vision

To help as many athletes as possible earn college opportunities through a trusted development program that prepares them for modern recruiting. We are building Gentle Giants: athletes with skill, character, confidence, and strength on and off the field.
